About

Built on 15 years working inside some of the largest major label-affiliated and independent merchandise companies on the planet. Piecemakers applies that expertise in a boutique, artist-first model — without the inflated price tag.

What We Do
  • A boutique approach to creative world-building through physical products
  • White-glove service with a focus on quality over rapid scaling
  • Prioritization of design and deep understanding of the artist’s brand
  • No hidden specialty fees that cut into artists’ profits
  • Flexible, short-term agreements instead of restrictive long-term contracts
  • Full transparency in pricing, production, and partnerships
  • Artists retain full ownership and access to their merchandise
  • Full control over likeness and merchandise rights
